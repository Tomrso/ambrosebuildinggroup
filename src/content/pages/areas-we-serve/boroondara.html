Building & Renovating Across Boroondara

Outdoor Entertainment Areas In Boroondara

Outdoor work in Boroondara is rarely a stand-alone deck stuck in the middle of the yard. The brief is typically about extending the home's living areas out into a garden that's already established, integrating finishes and detailing so the new work looks like it was always there.

Period homes through Hawthorn, Camberwell and Kew often have generous side returns or rear gardens that suit a covered alfresco zone with a proper outdoor kitchen. Glen Iris and Ashburton homes lean toward decks and pergolas that bridge between the kitchen and the back garden. Detail and finish matter more here than in most parts of Melbourne.

Pool Houses In Boroondara

The bigger Boroondara blocks across Kew, Balwyn, Canterbury and parts of Camberwell are where pool house projects make sense. A standalone pool house turns the pool zone into a complete outdoor living area, with covered seating, a change room, a bathroom and often a small kitchen or bar.

The build needs to match the standard of the main house, both in detailing and material choice. We design and construct pool houses to read as part of the original property rather than a flatpack stuck next to the pool.

Learn more about custom pool houses →

Luxury Renovations In Boroondara

Boroondara is renovation territory. Edwardians, Federations and Californian Bungalows make up a huge proportion of the housing stock and most need internal modernisation to suit how families live now while keeping the period detail intact at the front of the home.

Most renovation jobs here pair a kitchen and main bathroom upgrade with an opening of the rear of the home toward the garden. Butler's pantries, mudrooms, restored period details at the front of the home, full kitchens and ensuites at the back. Heritage overlays through parts of Hawthorn, Kew and Camberwell mean the planning side has to be handled carefully.
