Building & Renovating Across Manningham

Outdoor Entertainment Areas In Manningham

Manningham backyards are typically larger than the rest of the Eastern Suburbs and the briefs reflect that. Big covered alfresco zones, full outdoor kitchens, and decks designed to take in the views and space across Templestowe and Park Orchards.

The semi-rural blocks toward Warrandyte and Donvale often have mature trees and natural slope to work around, which makes the design phase critical. Doncaster and Bulleen homes tend to have flatter, more conventional yards but still with plenty of room to build out a proper entertaining zone.

Every project starts with a site inspection so the design suits the property, the home and the way you want to use the space.

Pool Houses In Manningham

Manningham is the right council for pool house work. The bigger lots through Templestowe, Park Orchards, Warrandyte and parts of Doncaster have the room for a standalone pool house that does more than store pool equipment.

The brief typically includes covered seating, a change room, a bathroom and sometimes an outdoor kitchen or bar setup. Designed and built to match the existing home so the whole backyard reads as one cohesive outdoor living zone, not a flatpack stuck next to the pool.

Luxury Renovations In Manningham

A lot of Manningham housing stock is 1970s to 1990s mid-century and contemporary brick veneer. The bones are typically excellent but the kitchens, bathrooms and internal layouts often need a full overhaul to suit how families live now.

Most renovation jobs here start with the kitchen and a main bathroom and expand from there. Knocking through to open up the living areas, adding a butler's pantry, redoing laundries, upgrading the master suite. Done properly, the result is a home that lives like a new build but holds onto the warmth and character of the original.
