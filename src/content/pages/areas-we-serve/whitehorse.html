Building & Renovating Across Whitehorse

Outdoor Entertainment Areas In Whitehorse

Most Whitehorse backyards have something already there. A tired pergola, an old timber deck that's seen better days, a paved area that gets too hot in summer. The job is usually working out what to keep, what to pull down and how to put together something that finally suits the home.

The leafy streets through Blackburn, Mont Albert and Surrey Hills tend to have established gardens and mature trees, so decks and alfresco spaces need to be planned around what's already there. Newer Vermont South and Box Hill North homes often have larger flat backyards and bigger entertaining briefs, covered alfresco zones, outdoor kitchens, the lot.

Whatever the starting point, every project goes through a proper site inspection so the design suits how you actually use the space.

Pool Houses In Whitehorse

The bigger blocks in Surrey Hills, Mont Albert, Box Hill South and Vermont South are where most of our pool house work happens, with the room to do it properly. A standalone pool house turns the pool zone into a real outdoor living area instead of a pool with deck chairs around it.

The brief usually includes some combination of covered seating, a change room, a bathroom and somewhere to keep pool equipment out of sight. We design and build them to match the existing house so the whole backyard reads as one cohesive space, not a Bunnings flatpack stuck next to the pool.

Luxury Renovations In Whitehorse

A huge proportion of Whitehorse housing stock is 50+ years old. Postwar brick veneers right through Mitcham, Nunawading and Vermont, Edwardian and Federation homes around Box Hill, Blackburn and Mont Albert. The kitchens are tight, the bathrooms are dated and the layouts don't suit how people live now.

Most renovation jobs in Whitehorse start with the kitchen and a main bathroom, then expand out from there. Knocking through to open up the living areas, adding a butler's pantry, redoing the laundry, getting the floors running consistently. Done properly, the result is a home that lives like a new build but keeps the character that drew you to it in the first place.

We handle the whole project through one licensed builder so the trades and timelines actually line up.
