Building & Renovating Across Monash

Outdoor Entertainment Areas In Monash

Outdoor work in Monash splits into two camps. New contemporary builds across Glen Waverley and Wheelers Hill where the brief is a fully integrated alfresco zone designed alongside the home, and established postwar properties where the existing yard has been underused for decades and the upgrade transforms the way the family lives.

Mt Waverley and Mulgrave homes typically have generous backyards that easily accommodate a covered alfresco zone with an outdoor kitchen. Tighter Oakleigh and Clayton blocks reward smarter design that gets the most out of available space without making the area feel cramped.

Pool Houses In Monash

The bigger blocks in Wheelers Hill, Mulgrave and parts of Mt Waverley are where most pool house projects in Monash come from. The pool sits clear of the house and there's actually room to do a standalone pool house properly with covered seating, a change room, a bathroom and storage.

The build needs to match the existing home so the entire backyard reads as one cohesive outdoor living space, not a flatpack stuck next to the pool.

Luxury Renovations In Monash

A huge proportion of Monash housing stock is postwar through to early 1990s. Original kitchens, dated bathrooms, layouts that worked in 1975 and don't work now. Outdoor areas tacked on at some point that have never quite functioned. The bones are typically excellent and the renovation pays back many times over in livability and value.

Most renovation jobs in Monash start with the kitchen and main bathroom and expand from there. Knocking through to open up the living areas, adding a butler's pantry, redoing the laundry, upgrading ensuites. Glen Waverley sees more knockdown rebuild activity than other parts of the council, but a careful renovation is often a better answer if the house has good bones.
