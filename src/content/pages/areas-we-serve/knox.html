Building & Renovating Across Knox

Outdoor Entertainment Areas In Knox

Knox backyards are bigger than most. The 1970s and 1980s family homes through Wantirna, Knoxfield and Boronia were built on generous blocks and the briefs we see usually take advantage of that. Proper covered alfresco zones, full outdoor kitchens, decks built around the way the family actually uses the yard rather than tacked on as an afterthought.

Newer Rowville and Wantirna South homes often have flatter, more contemporary backyards that suit large open decks and integrated outdoor kitchens. Hilly blocks toward Ferntree Gully and the Dandenongs reward smarter design that handles the slope and uses the elevation as a feature.

Pool Houses In Knox

Knox is one of the strongest councils in our service area for pool house work. Big family blocks across Rowville, Wantirna South, Knoxfield and parts of Wantirna give the pool zone enough room to be treated as a complete outdoor living area rather than just a fenced pool with a couple of chairs around it.

The brief usually includes covered seating, a change room, a bathroom and somewhere proper to keep pool equipment out of sight. We design and build them to match the existing home so the entire backyard reads as one cohesive space.

Luxury Renovations In Knox

A huge proportion of Knox housing stock is between 30 and 50 years old. Family homes that suited a young family in 1985 but feel tight, dated and disconnected by today's standards. The bones are typically excellent and the upgrade pays back in livability.

Most renovation jobs in Knox start with the kitchen and main bathroom and expand from there. Knocking through to open up the living areas, adding a butler's pantry, redoing the laundry, upgrading ensuites. Done properly, the result is a home that lives like a new build but holds onto the warmth of the original.
