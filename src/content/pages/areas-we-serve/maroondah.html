Luxury Renovations In Maroondah

Maroondah's housing stock skews toward family homes built between the 1960s and 1990s. The kitchens are usually closed off, the bathrooms are dated, and the laundries were tucked into a corner that no longer works. The bones tend to be solid which makes them ideal renovation candidates.

Most renovation jobs in Maroondah start with the kitchen and a main bathroom and spread out from there. Knocking through to open up the living areas, adding a butler's pantry, redoing the laundry, getting the floors running consistently. The end result is a home that lives like a new build but holds onto everything that made it the right choice in the first place.
